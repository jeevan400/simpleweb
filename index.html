Kuch Baatein Jo Kehni Thi

Main perfect nahi hoon, yeh baat main jaanta hoon. Lekin itna zaroor keh sakta hoon ki jo bhi mehsoos karta hoon, woh dil se karta hoon… aur aapke liye jo hai, woh bilkul sachcha aur saaf hai.

Jab se aap meri zindagi mein aayi hain, sab kuch dheere dheere badalne laga hai… aur sach kahun to yeh change mere liye bahut khoobsurat hai.

Main bas itna chahta hoon ki mujhe ek mauka mile — aapki har khushi ka hissa banne ka, aur hamesha aapka saath nibhane ka. 🌹
